Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

Buildup One (“the app”) is a personal strength-training log. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It is short on purpose, because the app is built to keep your data on your device.

1.What we collect: nothing

The developer of Buildup One does not collect, receive, transmit or store any personal information about you. There is no account and no sign-in, and no user profile exists on any server. We run no servers that receive your data.

2.Where your data lives

Everything you enter — workouts, exercises, weights, notes, your unit and language preferences and your opal progress — is stored only in a local database on your device. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (see “Backups”). We cannot see it.

3.No tracking, no analytics, no ads

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking components. It does not use your advertising identifier. It works fully offline.

4.Backups

You can create a backup file of your data from within the app and restore from one later. These files are written to your device’s storage. You decide where they go and whether to move or share them (for example, to a new phone). We are not involved in that process and never receive the files.

5.Payments

Buildup One is offered as a paid subscription through Google Play. Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Google. We do not see or store your payment card details, billing address or Google account. Google’s handling of that information is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

6.Permissions

The app requests only the storage access needed to write and read backup files and, for subscriptions, the standard Google Play billing permission. It does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone or anything similar.

7.Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because it collects nothing, it holds nothing about children or anyone else.

8.Your control

Because your data is only on your device, you are always in full control of it. You can edit or delete any entry in the app, and uninstalling the app removes its local database and settings. There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we hold nothing.

9.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. If a future version of the app adds anything that collects or shares data (for example, an ad-supported free version), this policy will be updated to describe it before that version is released.

10.Disclaimer and limitation of liability

Buildup One is a tool for recording your own training. It is not medical, health or fitness advice and is not a substitute for guidance from a qualified professional. Strength training carries a risk of injury. You use the app, and perform any exercise, at your own risk; if you are unsure whether an exercise is safe for you, consult a doctor or a qualified trainer first.

The exercise names, descriptions and “how-to” steps in the app are brief, general information. They may be incomplete, may not suit your equipment or ability, and are not personal instruction.

The app is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind, express or implied. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the developer of Buildup One is not liable for any injury, data loss, lost profit, or any direct, indirect, incidental or consequential damages arising from your use of, or inability to use, the app.

Nothing here limits any rights you have under mandatory consumer-protection law in your country that cannot be waived by agreement.
